A Data Science & AI Approach
to Real Estate

Every acquisition is evaluated through a quantitative lens — the same data science and artificial intelligence toolkit I use to build production ML systems, translated into underwriting that outperforms the retail benchmark.

i. Market Selection

ML-driven submarket scoring across rent growth, vacancy, supply pipeline, and job diversification. The goal is to enter cycles early, not follow consensus.

ii. Deal Underwriting

Probabilistic cash-flow modeling with sensitivity on rents, CapEx, financing, and exit. Every deal is stress-tested by AI-assisted simulation before it is bid.

iii. Operating Edge

Ongoing performance tracking, AI-driven pricing for short-term rentals, and data-driven CapEx sequencing to compound returns over the hold.
